# Declutter first (Oct 22, 2026)

Caleb, after the kid usability sessions: "DECLUTTER DECLUTTER DECLUTTER. This is non-negotiable. Any section that just
has too many words must be reduced. I don't care if we have to cut features; prioritize clarity, intuition and
accessibility of core features." Later: "Clarity, less is more, user intuition."

**Why:** four kid testers froze on a busy Home, skipped wordy steps and ignored quotes; they went straight for the
dish photo, the video and the cook's voice. Every extra word or control competed with "start cooking".

**How to apply:**
- Before adding anything to a screen, remove something. A feature that makes a screen wordy gets cut or hidden behind
  one tap (collapsed tip, 2-line substeps with "…").
- Never fix confusion by adding another pill, icon or explanation.
- Short labels, amounts before words, one primary action per screen. Video and voice above text.
- This beats older rules like "the cook's words are always shown": on kid screens, ingredients are amount + name only.

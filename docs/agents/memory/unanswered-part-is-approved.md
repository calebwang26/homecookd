On 2026-10-24 Caleb said: "if i dont reply to a section, that means its approved."

When he answers a proposal with several parts (options, steps, a list of fixes), any part he doesn't mention is
approved as proposed.

**Why:** he reviews fast and answers only what he wants changed. Asking again about the parts he skipped wastes
his time and stalls the work.

**How to apply:**
- After his reply, build the parts he changed his way and the parts he skipped as proposed. Don't ask about the
  skipped parts again.
- Say plainly in the wrap-up which parts went ahead as proposed because he didn't comment, so he can stop one.
- This covers silence on a part of something he replied to. It isn't approval for anything he never saw, and it
  never covers deploys, deletions, money or messages sent for him.

Caleb's lander review on 2026-10-13 set these rules for every lander copy & demo change:

- **Two ICPs:** (1) students about to start college who are cooking for themselves for the first time & miss their family; (2) families who want to pass recipes down to their kids. Since Oct 13 each ICP gets its own lander version; the problem lists live in [[lander-icp-problems]].
- **Three pain points** to name: kids have no time; parents & kids live far apart; the language barrier. (This replaces the Oct 12 framing in [[lander-design-references]] that called vague recipes a problem.)
- **Sell emotional intelligence, not technical complexity.** Connection across distance, culture & heritage passed down, love both ways. Copy that only describes features ("Prop up a phone… Edible turns it into a recipe") misses: show the feature *inside* the emotional pain.
- **The user path is parent → kid:** the parent records their own cooking, adds the videos & sends it to the kid. It is not "you record them".
- **Instantly intuitive or cut it.** He was lost by clever devices (a cross-out + "not anymore" + "a handful" with no context). Each section must make what Edible does obvious in a few seconds, in a fun way. A section that doesn't plainly connect to the product or the brand ("Every family calls them something") reads as useless.
- **Demo pacing:** visitors give each demo step about 3 seconds. App clips should show one clear action (adding the recipe & videos, checking off ingredients, tapping Watch to see the clip + transcript), not long setups like prompts.
- **Keep UI he likes and change only the words** when he says so; he often explicitly says "don't change a single drop of the UI structure".
- Photos must show clearly what's happening (people visibly making dumplings, not a kid standing there). No em dash or rule inside button labels.

**Why:** the lander must make a first-time visitor *feel* the problem & understand the product immediately; it's a family product, not a tool.
**How to apply:** check every lander copy or demo change against these points before showing it to him.

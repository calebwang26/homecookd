# DIRECTION: the creative director's master brief for the next homecookd app

Read BRIEF.md first (facts, hard rules, file format). This file tells you **what to feel, what to say,
what to show and how each concept looks**. Where this file and AUDIENCE-AND-SEEDS.md differ, this file
wins. Where this file and BRIEF.md section 7-8 (hard rules, format) differ, BRIEF.md wins.

The one sentence behind everything: **homecookd is how a family keeps cooking together from two kitchens.**
Teaching is how Mom says I love you. Asking, cooking it and sending a photo back is how Leo says it back.
Every screen must make one of those two gestures easier, and feel like it.

## A. Audience truths

### Mom (late 50s, the cook)
- **Who she is.** Has cooked the family's food for thirty years without a recipe. Uses her phone for
  calls, photos, the family group chat and video calls with Leo. Avoids forms and settings. Reads with
  her glasses pushed up, so small grey text is invisible to her. Proud of her cooking, quietly unsure it
  is "fancy enough" to record.
- **What she feels opening the app.** A small hope that Leo has been there: did he watch it, did he cook
  it, did he ask her something. Underneath: she misses him and is afraid of being a bother.
- **What she fears.** Pressing the wrong thing and breaking it. Not knowing if something sent. Being
  asked to type. Looking silly on camera. Her English. That nobody will use what she made. That the
  family's food ends with her.
- **What makes her tear up (good tears).** A photo of Leo's first bowl of her ramen with "Tasted like
  home." Seeing that he watched the folding part three times. Leo asking for "the eggs you made the
  morning I left." Hearing her own voice teaching, and realizing it is kept. "Kept for Leo, for Maya,
  and for their kids someday."
- **What loses her.** Icons without words. Anything that needs a swipe or a long press. Two buttons that
  look equally important. Grey 13px text. Words like upload, sync, processing, account. A screen with
  six things on it. Not knowing how to go back. A spinner with no sentence.

### Leo (19, the one who misses it)
- **Who he is.** First time cooking alone. A tiny shared kitchen, one pot, one pan. Busy, broke, tired,
  usually cooking after 9 pm. Grew up eating Mom's dumplings every Sunday and never learned how.
  Calls home less than he means to.
- **What he feels opening the app.** Homesick in a way he doesn't say out loud. He wants something warm
  that tastes like home, fast and cheap, and he wants to hear her.
- **What he fears.** Wasting money on groceries and ruining it. That it won't taste like hers. Drifting
  away from his family. That Nai Nai's recipes disappear. That cooking takes too long on a school night.
- **What makes him tear up (good tears).** Mom's voice saying his name. "Saved you a bag for
  Thanksgiving." Nai Nai, in Mandarin, explaining why you never cut a noodle. Mom's delight at the
  photo he sent. The sound of the pan.
- **What loses him.** A generic recipe app (if it looks like a food blog, the magic is gone). Walls of
  text. Unclear time and cost. Five taps before he can cook. Anything that guilt-trips him about
  calling home. Cheesy mascots everywhere.

## B. Emotional intelligence playbook (the app knows them)

**Principle.** The app notices one true, specific thing and offers one small action. It never lectures,
never counts absence at the parent, never makes anyone sad on purpose. Specific beats generic every
time: "Midterms this week. Mom's ramen takes 10 minutes." beats "Recommended for you."

| Signal | Kid line (where) | Parent line (where) |
|---|---|---|
| It's Sunday (dumpling day at home) | "Sunday at home means dumplings. Here are yours." (Kid-Home greeting or hero line) | "It's Sunday. What would you like to teach today?" (only on a Sunday screen; Parent-Send is Sunday) |
| Exam week | "Midterms this week. Mom's ramen takes 10 minutes." (Picked for you reason) | "Leo has midterms this week. Something warm would help." (Parent-Home context line, optional) |
| Late night | "Still up? This one takes 10 minutes." (Lamplight greeting) | **Never shown.** Mom never learns he was up at 2 am. |
| Time away | "7 weeks away. Here's a little Sunday." (kid only, at most once) | **Never count absence at Mom.** Say "Leo comes home for Thanksgiving soon." |
| Mom's birthday, Oct 27 | "Mom's birthday is in 2 weeks. Bake her banana bread and send a photo?" (Kid-Home-Scrolled) | **Never shown.** The app keeps his surprise. |
| He just cooked her recipe | "Send Mom a photo. She'll want to see." (after cooking, or as a hint on Kid-Recipe) | "Leo made your ramen." + his photo + his note (Parent-Home) |
| He keeps rewatching a step | (n/a) | "Leo watched the folding part twice. Want to add a tip?" (Parent-Home-Scrolled, small) |
| A question is waiting | "Mom answered. Hear her." (Kid-Home-Scrolled) | "Leo asked you something." + big "Answer with your voice" (Parent-Home or Parent-Recipe) |
| Nai Nai's voice is kept | "Nai Nai's voice, kept for always." (Hear their voices) | "Nai Nai's pork belly is kept here too, in her own voice." (legacy line) |
| He's broke | "About $2 a bowl." / "Makes 6 bowls. Lunch all week." | (n/a) |
| First time | "First dumplings? Make 10 tonight, freeze the rest." | "It's fine if it's messy. Real kitchens are." |
| He asked to learn | (n/a) | "Leo asked for your Sunday Eggs. 'The ones you made the morning I left.'" |

**Rules.**
1. Warm and hopeful. Every context line ends in an invitation, not a fact that stings.
2. Never guilt. Banned: "You haven't called Mom in…", "Mom misses you" as pressure, "Don't let her
   recipes disappear", "before it's too late", anything about death or getting old.
3. Specific over generic: a day, a dish, a person, a minute count. Never "your content", "recommended".
4. Connect the feature to the feeling in the same line: "Hear Mom" not "Play audio"; "Answer with your
   voice" not "Record response".
5. One context line per region, two per screen at most. If every card is "smart", none of them is.
6. Privacy of the heart: the kid side may know Mom's birthday; the parent side never sees the kid's
   late nights, skipped meals or how long since he called.

## C. Voice and COPY BANK

### Voice
- Short, warm, plain. Headers 2-5 words. Sentences under 12 words where possible.
- Talk like family: "Hear Mom", "Leo asked you something", "Saved you a bag".
- Kid voice: a little playful, budget- and time-aware. Parent voice: respectful, the expert in the room,
  never jokey about phones, always reassuring.
- The cook's own words are always shown, in quotes, in Fraunces italic. Estimates always carry "~".
- One exclamation mark per screen at most (most screens: zero).
- **Banned words on screen:** upload, sync, AI, process/processing, content, generate, account, user,
  profile, settings, notifications, submit, feed, curated, personalized, smart, data, transcript, media,
  file(s), playback, swipe, long-press, tap and hold. Say instead: send, add your videos, her words,
  we'll let you know, your recipe will be ready by tonight.
- **No em dash, en dash or slash inside button labels.** No "Click here".

## D. Clarity rules (for the older parent, and the tired kid at 11 pm)

**Checklist every screen must pass**
1. **One primary action**, and it is the biggest, most colorful thing below the hero. Everything else is
   visibly secondary (outline, tint or text).
2. **Max 3 sections visible** (parent: 2). Each section starts with a 26-32px header.
3. **Words on every icon.** Tab icons have words. Icon-only buttons (back, heart, share) are allowed only
   on photos in kid versions and need `aria-label`; parent back buttons show "Back".
4. **No gestures required.** Nothing depends on swipe, long-press or pinch. Horizontal rows (kid only)
   must show a clear peek of the next card AND a round arrow button in the header.
5. **No hidden menus.** No "…" overflow, no hamburger.
6. **Predictable back.** Top-left, 44px+ round button (kid) or a pill with chevron + "Back" (parent).
7. **Selected states light up** (ref 2): 2.5-3px colored outline + a filled check circle, or a filled
   block + check. Unselected: hairline border, no check, muted.
8. **Plain words** (see banned list). Numbers in digits ("3 times", "10 min").
9. **Contrast:** text ≥ 4.5:1; ≥ 3:1 only at 24px+. White text on photos only over a scrim
   (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(0,0,0,0) 35%, rgba(18,20,16,0.78) 100%)` or the concept's ground
   color). Verified pairs: cream on #5B6F00 5.55, cream on #4C5A10 7.43, cream on #34430C 10.6,
   #D1E043 on #34430C 7.4, #1E2410 on #FEBE29 9.6, #5F6352 on peach/sage/butter/canvas ≥ 4.99,
   cream on #C8462A 4.73. **Fails, do not use for text under 24px:** cream on tomato #E95D3D (3.39),
   #9A4F47 on rose (4.27), #C8462A on peach (3.88) or kraft (3.55), leaf #788C15 on cream (3.71),
   sun #FEBE29 on anything light.
10. **Touch targets** ≥ 44px (parent ≥ 56px). Buttons ≥ 52px tall (parent ≥ 60, primary 64-72).
11. **Every voice/video control says whose and how long:** "Hear Mom · 0:24".
12. **Status is a sentence**, never a spinner: "Sent to Leo. His recipe will be ready by tonight."
13. **Nothing touches the tab bar.** Leave ≥ 16px between the last content and the bar's top edge;
    content that runs under the bar must be visibly cut by it (that's the peek).
14. **Caveat is decoration.** It never carries information that isn't also in DM Sans or Fraunces, and
    is never under 22px.

## G. Review rubric

Score each criterion 1-5, multiply by its weight. Max 100. **Pass = 80+, no criterion below 3, zero
auto-fails.** Review the 9 boards of a concept together on one contact sheet first, then each board.

| Criterion | Weight | 5 looks like | Specific fails to look for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Emotion** | ×5 | A 60-year-old mom would feel pride and a homesick 19-year-old would feel held. Mom's voice is one tap away. Context lines are specific (exam week, Sunday, 6:15 pm). The note from Mom/Leo reads like a real family. | Generic recipe-app copy ("Recommended", "Trending"). Guilt or sadness as a lever. Context lines on every card. The kid side missing Mom's voice above the fold. Parent side not showing anything from Leo above the fold. No faces anywhere. |
| **Clarity** | ×5 | The primary action is obvious in 2 seconds. Headers stand out. Tab bar with words, active tab unmistakable. Selected states light up. Parent version visibly bigger and simpler. | Two equally loud buttons. More than 3 sections (parent 2). Text under 15px (tab labels under 12). Grey-on-tint failing contrast. Icon-only actions on parent screens. Horizontal scroll with no peek/arrow. Content hidden behind the tab bar. A "…" menu. |
| **References fidelity** | ×3 | All 8 structures are present across the 9 screens exactly where this file puts them (Subway hero, ref 2 option boxes, ref 3 tiles + buttons + segmented bar, Luma status pills, Netflix profiles, Google TV hero, DICE rows, DICE bubble panels with hearts). | Profiles not first or not split into cooks / kids. No bubble panel with hearts. Browse rows without photo-left / heart-right / accent line / muted line. Recipe without the segmented white card. Copied brand colors from the references. |
| **Brand fit** | ×3 | Unmistakably homecookd: Fraunces SOFT, DM Sans, Caveat used sparingly, our tokens, round shapes, the pot, and clearly THIS concept (not drifting toward another concept's signature). | Inter/Roboto/system font look. Off-palette colors beyond the 2 allowed new hexes. AI tropes: decorative gradients, left-border accent cards, glassmorphism, emoji. Two concepts that look alike. |
| **Craft** | ×4 | Pixel-tidy spacing on an 8pt rhythm, no clipping, crops centered on faces and food, consistent radii, real copy everywhere, prototype links work, render.py prints no warnings. | Clipped or overflowing text. Faces cut at the eyes. Photos stretched. Stickers covering titles. Lorem or "[placeholder]". Fake status bar or notch. Broken links or missing `aria-label` on icon-only buttons. |

**Auto-fails (any one = redo the board):**
- An em dash, en dash or slash in a button label; a banned word on screen; more than one "!" per screen.
- A person photo cast as someone it isn't (e.g. `photo-cook-recording-phone` as Mom, the son in
  `photo-dad-son-cooking` shown at all, two different photos as the same person on one screen).
- Mom's own screens mentioning time since Leo was home, or Leo's late nights; Mom's birthday anywhere on
  the parent side.
- White text directly on a photo with no scrim.
- Missing tab bar on a main screen, or a tab bar without words.
- Format violations from BRIEF.md section 8 (classes, `url()` backgrounds, wrong blob urls, missing the
  dc script block, root not 390×844).
- Facts that contradict section 0 (wrong dish on a screen, invented stats like "1,000 families").

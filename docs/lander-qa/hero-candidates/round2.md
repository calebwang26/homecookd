# Hero video, round 2 (Oct 13, 2026)

## The brief

1. A young-adult child (about 18–30) cooking with their parent.
2. Quality at least as good as c1 (Pexels 4823202): native 4K, tack-sharp, warm.
3. The food being made clearly in frame: medium or wide shots, not two faces up close.

## Search

Two scouts searched in parallel.

**Pexels.** About 150 queries returned around 3,000 results. Of those, about 1,100 were UHD clips; about 300 posters and about 40 previews were reviewed. Only one shoot meets all three requirements: Gustavo Fring's family-kitchen shoot (7423603, 7423605).
- Runner-up: 9058217, SHVETS. The footage looks premium, but they're making coffee, not cooking, and the dad looks about 68.
- Rejected: everything else was a small child, a grandparent, posed footage, 1080p only, or hands only.

**Mixkit, Pixabay, Coverr.** Nothing meets the bar.
- Free parent and older-child clips are 1080p only (Coverr).
- Or they show small kids, or dinner-table shots with no cooking (Mixkit).
- Or the license is restricted (Mixkit's Christmas set).
- The 1080p Coverr near-misses are soft compared with c1.

## Pick: Gustavo Fring, Pexels 7423603 + 7423605

**Why this one**
- It's the only native-4K clip anywhere with a parent and young-adult kids cooking together.
- The laugh is real, and the food is in frame in every shot.
- It matches both audiences: parents of 50–60 and kids in their twenties.

**The edit**
- 0.3–10.5 s of 7423603, the medium laugh.
- A hard cut to 13.4–17.3 s of 7423605, the wide shot: she slices tomatoes, and the daughter turns and smiles.
- A hard cut back to the start. The loop seam is a change of camera angle, like the previous hero's, so there's no crossfade ghosting.
- Grade: a gentle warm-up (colortemperature 5200 K at 60% mix, contrast 1.08, saturation 1.12). The source grade is flat and cool. Skin warms without going orange.
- Phone crop: a slightly tighter 9:16 window, set low enough that the mom's and daughter's faces sit in the top third of the screen, above the headline. On phones the H1 starts 38% down. For the wide shot the window centers on the mom slicing at the board.
